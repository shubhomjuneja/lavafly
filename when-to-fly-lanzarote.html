WHEN TO FLY IN LANZAROTE

Understanding Lanzarote's Flying Conditions

Lanzarote offers flying potential throughout much of the year, but tandem paragliding depends on daily wind direction, wind strength and atmospheric stability—not the calendar alone. Understanding trade winds and coastal ridge lift helps identify the most favorable periods.

Seasonal Overview

🌱 Spring (March – May)

Often balanced: stable airflow, moderate wind speeds and comfortable temperatures. A strong all-round season for tandem paragliding in Lanzarote.

☀️ Summer (June – September)

During summer, trade winds are typically consistent, the air is dry and clear, and visibility is often excellent. Ridge lift conditions can be strong and dynamic, offering powerful coastal flying potential and spectacular panoramic views.

Daily conditions are assessed professionally to determine the most suitable flight window.

🍂 Autumn (October – November)

Often combines warm conditions with stable patterns. Many pilots consider it one of the most reliable periods for scenic ridge flights, especially in Famara.

❄️ Winter (December – February)

Mild compared to mainland Europe. Suitable flying days occur depending on wind direction and local weather systems.

What Actually Determines Daily Flight Conditions

Key daily factors include wind alignment with launch sites, wind speed range, gust factor, turbulence potential and topographical airflow effects. This is why professional forecast checks and on-site evaluation are essential.

What Should Passengers Wear?

Even on warm days, it can feel cooler in the air. We recommend warm clothing depending on the season, a light jacket or windproof layer, and closed shoes.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Weather & Timing Questions

WHAT IS THE BEST MONTH FOR PARAGLIDING IN LANZAROTE?

Spring and autumn are often stable, but flyable days occur throughout much of the year depending on wind direction and conditions.

CAN YOU FLY IN SUMMER?

Yes, depending on wind direction and strength. Summer trade winds often provide excellent conditions for coastal ridge flying.

IS WINTER SUITABLE FOR PARAGLIDING?

Yes, when conditions align. Lanzarote's mild winter climate means suitable flying days are possible throughout the season.

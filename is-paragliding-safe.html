Tandem Paragliding Safety in Lanzarote

Tandem paragliding in Lanzarote is safe when operated professionally: certified pilots, modern equipment, and strict weather limits. Safety is built on experience, discipline, structured planning and responsible decision-making.

How Tandem Safety Works

In tandem flights, the pilot is fully responsible for launch, flight control and landing. The passenger does not steer or make technical decisions, which significantly reduces complexity for first-time flyers.

This is what makes tandem paragliding in Lanzarote accessible to virtually anyone — you fly with a professional who manages everything.

Weather Evaluation (Core Safety Factor)

Before every flight, professionals assess wind direction, wind speed, gust levels, turbulence potential and topographical airflow effects. Flights are conducted only when conditions fall within strict safety limits. If conditions are not suitable, flights are postponed.

Why Lanzarote Offers Stable Flying Conditions

Lanzarote benefits from consistent trade winds, coastal ridge lift, wide landing areas and clear terrain visibility. Ridge lift areas often provide smoother airflow than strong thermic mountain environments.

The Famara cliffs are one of the strongest examples of this reliable coastal ridge flying in the Canary Islands.

Adapted Flight Planning and Individual Assessment

At LavaFly Lanzarote, we go beyond standard procedures when necessary. Our team has experience in structured individual assessment and adapted preparation. When specific physical or medical considerations exist, feasibility and safety are evaluated before confirming a flight—always with safety as the absolute priority.

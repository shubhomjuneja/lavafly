Tandem Paragliding in Lanzarote

Tandem paragliding in Lanzarote is one of the most unforgettable outdoor experiences in the Canary Islands. The island's volcanic landscapes, dramatic coastline and Atlantic trade winds create ideal conditions for smooth, scenic coastal flights.

Unlike many mountain destinations that rely on seasonal thermals, Lanzarote offers reliable coastal ridge lift across the island when wind direction aligns with the launch site. This makes tandem paragliding accessible for beginners while still delivering the thrill and beauty that adventure travelers look for.

No previous experience is required. You fly securely connected to a certified professional pilot who manages takeoff, flight control and landing—while you relax and enjoy the view.

Why Lanzarote Is Ideal for Tandem Paragliding

Lanzarote benefits from consistent trade winds and coastal terrain that supports ridge lift. Key advantages include:

  • Predictable wind-driven lift (less dependent on thermals)
  • Panoramic coastal routes with open horizons
  • Mild climate and strong visibility many days of the year
  • Multiple possible flying areas depending on wind direction

This combination is why paragliding in Famara and across Lanzarote is often described as smooth, scenic and surprisingly peaceful—especially for first-time flyers.

What to Expect During Your Tandem Paragliding Experience

1 Arrival and Detailed Briefing (Assistant-led)

Upon arrival, our assistant personally walks you through every step in detail. This includes:

  • Equipment explanation and harness fitting
  • Safety positioning and passenger role
  • Takeoff technique and timing
  • Landing procedure and instructions
  • Wind and weather overview for the day

We take our time and answer every question before launch—nothing feels rushed.

2 Takeoff

Takeoff is simple and controlled. You walk forward together with your pilot while the wing inflates above you. Within seconds, you lift gently into the air. The sensation is smooth and natural.

3 The Flight (20–60 minutes)

LavaFly offers flight durations between 20 and 60 minutes. The duration is selected by the customer in advance and depends on the chosen package, wind conditions and safety evaluation.

This flexibility allows you to choose a scenic panoramic glide or an extended coastal exploration—while maintaining strict operational safety standards. Find out more about the best time to fly in Lanzarote.

4 Landing

Landings are guided step-by-step. Your pilot gives clear instructions to ensure a smooth, controlled landing.

Who Can Fly Tandem in Lanzarote?

Tandem paragliding is suitable for most first-time flyers, couples and solo travelers. Basic mobility is typically required for takeoff and landing.

At LavaFly Lanzarote, we also take a personalized approach when additional planning is needed. Our team has experience assessing individual situations (including certain physical limitations or health-related considerations) and adapting preparation where appropriate. Each request is discussed personally to ensure safety, comfort and realistic expectations.

What Should I Wear?

We recommend comfortable clothing and closed shoes. Depending on the season, warm clothing is recommended—temperatures can feel cooler in the air, even on sunny days.

Why Paragliding in Lanzarote Feels Different from Many Other Destinations

This is not limited to Famara—it applies to multiple flying areas across Lanzarote. Many mainland destinations rely heavily on strong mountain thermals. Lanzarote's coastal environment often offers:

  • Wind-driven ridge lift and stable airflow patterns
  • Wide open coastal panoramas
  • A calm, gliding sensation rather than aggressive thermal climbing
